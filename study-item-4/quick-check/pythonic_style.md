Good Pythonic style uses snake_case and meaningful names

Bad:
- bar() -> not descriptive
- varName -> camelCase, not Pythonic
- VERYLONGVARNAME -> uppercase is typically used for constants

Acceptable:
- foobar -> readable but not very descriptive
- longvarname -> readable but not ideal

Good:
- foo_bar() -> follows snake_case and is clear

Too Long:
- really_very_long_var_name -> correct style but unnecessarily long
